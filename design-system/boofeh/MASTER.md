# BOOFEH Design System

## Direction

Playful editorial marketplace: warm cream canvas, saturated campus colors, strong navy outlines, imperfect rotations and tactile offset shadows.

## Tokens

- Ink `#17324D`
- Paper `#FFF8ED`
- Surface `#FFFDF8`
- Primary turquoise `#32D6C6`
- Accent pink `#FF6FAE`
- Accent orange `#FF8A3D`
- Supporting blue `#74A9FF`
- Radius: 14 / 22 / 32px
- Borders: 2px ink
- Shadows: 4px and 7px hard offset ink
- Spacing: 4/8px rhythm

## Typography

- Vazirmatn for Persian UI and content
- Arial bold for compact Latin labels and display marks
- Body minimum 16px on mobile forms; line height 1.7–2

## Components

- Buttons and controls are at least 44px tall.
- Primary actions use turquoise with an ink outline and hard shadow.
- Product cards use one playful visual move only: offset shadow plus subtle hover lift.
- Inline SVG icons share 1.8px round strokes.
- Forms always keep a visible label and inline status message.
- Modals and drawers provide explicit close controls and Escape support.

## Responsive

- 4-column products at desktop, 3 at small desktop, 2 at tablet, 1 at phone.
- Mobile uses a four-item labeled dock and reserves footer/bottom space.
- Horizontal scrolling is limited to filter chips; the page never scrolls sideways.

## Accessibility

- Body and functional text meet AA contrast on their surfaces.
- Focus rings are visible and color is never the only status cue.
- Reduced-motion disables ticker and transition movement.
- Disabled stock actions use both text and disabled semantics.
